Make it yours

These are your handwritten weekly amounts. Change them anytime; savings recalculate automatically from your budget start date.

✨ How rollover works: Each completed week adds that week's allowance to each envelope. Expenses reduce that envelope. Unspent balances remain available indefinitely. Changing a weekly allowance recalculates the entire history at the new rate. Start on the day you want your new budget to begin; past spending is not automatically imported.
